# Developmental Biology Experimental Design

Design developmental studies around biological stage rather than clock time alone,
with explicit parent, clutch, litter, embryo, organ, lineage, and cell hierarchies.

## Design Frame

- Define organism or model, developmental process, staging system, lineage or
  tissue, perturbation window, comparator, endpoint, and intended mechanism.
- Identify parent, mating, clutch or litter, embryo or offspring, organ, specimen,
  image, cell, and analysis units.
- Treat siblings from one parent, litter, clutch, culture, or organoid batch as
  clustered observations, not automatically independent replicates.
- Distinguish longitudinal observation from destructive stage-specific cohorts and
  balance cohorts across parent, batch, day, operator, and instrument.

Reuse `animal-physiology-experimental-design` when animal-level welfare and
allocation dominate, plus the shared biological design and statistics skills.

## Developmental Validity

- Use an explicit staging rubric, scorer training, blinding, agreement checks, and
  rules for asynchronous or ambiguous specimens.
- Separate developmental delay, altered fate, morphology, viability, migration,
  proliferation, differentiation, and patterning as distinct explanations.
- Record parental state, maternal or paternal effects, sex where relevant,
  temperature, nutrition, density, position, collection window, and handling.
- Use matched-stage and matched-time controls when needed to distinguish timing
  shifts from structural change.

## Ethics And Boundaries

Require applicable human-subject, embryo, animal, stem-cell, organoid, genetic,
biosafety, consent, and data approvals. Route operational work to authorized SOPs.
Do not provide executable manipulation, breeding, exposure, culture, or termination
parameters, and do not make clinical or reproductive decisions.

## Design Artifacts

Deliver a developmental hypothesis and staging plan; parent-to-cell unit hierarchy;
cohort and perturbation matrix; lineage and timing map; scorer and measurement-
validity plan; maternal-litter-clutch confounding audit; welfare and approval
register; and statistical handoff.

## Completion Criteria

Complete only when stage and time are distinguished, family clustering is
represented, destructive cohorts are valid, alternative developmental mechanisms
can be separated, and ethical and biosafety boundaries are explicit.
