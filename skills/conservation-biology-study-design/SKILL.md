# Conservation Biology Study Design

Design conservation studies backward from a management decision while preserving
the distinction between ecological evidence, value judgments, and authority.

## Decision And Design Frame

- Define conservation objective, target species or system, management decision,
  intervention, comparator, measurable endpoint, decision threshold, and time horizon.
- Identify population, site, management unit, habitat patch, individual, survey,
  detection, specimen, and analysis units.
- Select monitoring, before-after-control-impact, randomized intervention, matched
  comparison, natural experiment, demographic, or adaptive-management structure.
- State historical-baseline quality, intervention spillover, implementation fidelity,
  demographic lag, displacement, and external pressures.

Reuse `ecology-field-experimental-design` for sampling and detection and
`biological-study-statistics` for BACI contrasts, trends, uncertainty, and power.

## Validity And Decision Use

- Balance monitoring effort, observer, season, access, habitat, sensor, and method
  across intervention and comparison units.
- Separate occupancy, abundance, survival, recruitment, movement, habitat condition,
  genetic diversity, and ecosystem-function endpoints.
- Predefine how detectability, missing visits, extreme events, changing methods,
  and intervention noncompliance affect the decision.
- Report uncertainty and plausible alternative explanations; do not convert one
  surrogate endpoint into a population recovery claim without validation.

## Ethics And Governance

Require land, community, indigenous, protected-species, animal, collection,
biosecurity, and intervention authority. Minimize disturbance and protect sensitive
locations, cultural knowledge, enforcement data, and vulnerable populations. Do
not authorize capture, relocation, culling, release, habitat alteration, or other
management action.

## Design Artifacts

Deliver a decision and evidence map; management and sampling-unit hierarchy;
intervention and comparator matrix; BACI or monitoring timeline; detection and
effort plan; demographic and surrogate-endpoint definitions; spillover and
confounding audit; sensitive-data and authority register; and statistical handoff.

## Completion Criteria

Complete only when the study can inform the stated decision, management and
sampling units agree, detection and lag are addressed, intervention authority is
separate from analysis, and sensitive ecological data are protected.
