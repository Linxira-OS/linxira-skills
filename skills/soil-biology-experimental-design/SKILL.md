# Soil Biology Experimental Design

Plan soil studies so spatial heterogeneity, horizons, rhizosphere context,
compositing, and field-to-laboratory changes are part of the design.

## Sampling And Unit Structure

- Define soil system, biological question, land use, treatment or gradient, target
  depth or horizon, season, organism or process, endpoint, and inference scope.
- Identify site, field, plot, microsite, plant, rhizosphere or bulk soil, horizon,
  core, composite, aliquot, assay, and analysis units.
- State whether cores are independent samples, within-plot subsamples, or material
  pooled into a composite; a composite cannot recover its lost spatial variation.
- Record coordinates at an appropriate protection level, depth, horizon, moisture,
  temperature, texture, chemistry, vegetation, management, and recent disturbance.

Reuse `crop-plant-experimental-design` for plant and field treatment structure,
`ecology-field-experimental-design` for sampling, and shared statistics.

## Validity And Field-To-Lab Handoff

- Balance treatment across field, plot, slope, horizon, sampling date, collector,
  transport, storage, extraction, plate, instrument, and assay batch.
- Define field blanks, process blanks, reference soils, calibration samples,
  positive controls, and contamination controls according to the endpoint.
- Predefine compositing, homogenization, storage-state records, hold times under
  approved SOPs, freeze-thaw tracking, and sample-mass or moisture normalization.
- Separate microbial composition, biomass, activity, fauna, decomposition,
  nutrient pool, and process-rate claims.
- Audit treatment against soil chemistry, moisture, horizon, plant identity,
  topography, season, and management history.

## Safety And Access

Require land access, biosecurity, contaminated-site, agricultural chemical,
protected-area, transport, and controlled-location permissions. Route collection,
fumigation, extraction, organism handling, and waste to authorized SOPs. Do not
provide hazardous sampling or culture parameters.

## Design Artifacts

Deliver a soil sampling frame; site-plot-microsite-horizon-core hierarchy;
compositing map; field and laboratory batch plan; environmental metadata schema;
storage and chain-of-custody record; control matrix; spatial confounding audit;
permit register; and statistical handoff.

## Completion Criteria

Complete only when depth, horizon, microsite, compositing, spatial replication,
storage effects, environmental covariates, controls, and access boundaries support
the stated soil-biological inference.
